How Council uses your details

We only ask for what we need to deal with your report.

What we collect

Your name (if you give it), your email address, and the details and photo you add to your report.

What we use it for

To send you updates about this report and to ask questions if the team needs more detail. We don't use it for marketing.

Reporting anonymously

You can report without sharing any contact details. You won't get updates, and the report won't appear in My reports.
